== Introduction

Following winning the UK General Election in 2024 alot of 
https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-13871441/Keir-Starmer-justify-free-gift-worse-Labour-Baroness-Harman.html[press coverage appeared criticizing Labour MPs] for accepting donations and gifts from individuals and companies.
Most of the claims related to information in the 
https://www.parliament.uk/mps-lords-and-offices/standards-and-financial-interests/parliamentary-commissioner-for-standards/registers-of-interests/register-of-members-financial-interests/[Register of Members' Financial Interests]. This is available on the Houses of Parliament(HoP) website. 
It appears that at the start of this Parliamentary year they have mades some changes to what and how they release data.
All of this has been tracked by the folks at https://www.mysociety.org/[mySociety], the following articles will bring you 
up to speed on the changes and issue as well as some philosophical points about transparency being a hedge against reform.

* https://www.mysociety.org/2024/08/19/the-new-register-of-members-financial-interests-is-out-and-we-have-thoughts-and-spreadsheets/[The new Register of Members Financial Interests is out and we have thoughts (and spreadsheets)]

== First Look

As mySociety had already downloaded the files and created an Excel file available https://pages.mysociety.org/parl_register_interests/datasets/commons_rmfi/latest[here] and also provide a tool to explore the data - https://data.mysociety.org/datasette/?mysoc=parl_register_interests/commons_rmfi/latest#commons_rmfi[Explore data in Datasette].
As this allowed me to run SQL against the various files/tables I did my initial 'look' with this tool. I chose the Category_2
file which contained donations data. They had also added some columns to the original file, eg party.

=== Observations

The following seem to be the main ways the data is divided or sliced, I imagine these would be derived from a drop
down list or tick box in the data entry system.

* *category* - 'Donations and other support (including loans) for activities as an MP' same for all rows in file
* *memeber* - MPs names consistent in file, *mnis_id* seems to be a system internal identifier
* *donation_source* - Is either '(a) Support linked to an MP but received by a local party organisation or indirectly via a central party organisation' 
or '(b) Any other support not included in Category 2(a)' 
* *payment_type* - is ether 'Cash' or 'InKind'
* *donor_status* - is one of 'Company'(548), 'Individual'(1454), 'Limited Liability Partnership'(1), 'Other'(31), 'Registered Party'(12), 'Trade Union'(501), 'Trust'(6) or 'Unincorporated association'(64) with counts of each in brackets.

The following are the interesting ones and appear to be free text entries

* *value* - amount of money donated
* *payment_description* - free text description
* *donor_name* - name of donor
* *donor_company_identifier* - for companies the Companies House Number, this is a good addition, although a few are wrong, eg space in number or extra numbers added

The *donor_name* was the item that caught my attention the most following looking at Council Spends as entry appears to be free text there can be different versions 
of the same donor. An example of the issue is seen below. The variable spelling of Waheed Alli's name would generate 5 different aggregated counts for the same person, 
if this was corrected to a single *donor_name* in this case 'WAHEED ALLI(BARON ALLI) aggregations by donor would be reliable

== Next Steps
I decided to see if I could rationalise the various donor names to a consistent set mainly focusing on higher values of donations

. I loaded the HoP Category_2 file into a PostgreSQL table adding a new column upd_donor_name for the corrected donor name
. I had a 2 year old table of Companies House data in the database already and did a match by company number, that populated most of the high value donor companies.
I manually checked and updated the 30 or so companies that had not been matched
. The UK Government have an https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/official-list-of-trade-unions[Official list of trade unions] which I used to standardise the trade union donors
. The Individual donors was mainly a manual process of looking at the high value donors and reconciling the various spelling of their names to a standard set
. Companies, Individuals and Trade Unions comprise the bulk of all donors, I standardised one Unincorporated association
. I then exported the table from PostgreSQL and loaded it into Google Looker Studio(was Google Data Studio) and created a couple of Look ups by MP or Donor and a few visualisations
to try and get a better understanding of who donates to MPS.
